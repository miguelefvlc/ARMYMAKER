## 1. Army Special Rules

### Forward Artillery Doctrine
*Reference: Armies of France (v3) page 1*

The Artillery Forward Observer unit may call an artillery barrage twice instead of once per game. Resolve the first barrage as normal. Then, after the first barrage has been resolved, the observer may call a second (when issued a Fire order).

### Highly-Motivated Reserves
*Reference: Armies of France (v3) page 1*

Every Inexperienced unit that has one or more pin markers can make a test to lose one pin marker if there are any enemy units within 12" immediately before an order is given to it. Roll a die: on a roll of 4, 5 or 6 the test is passed and the unit loses one pin marker; on a 1, 2 or 3 the test is failed and the unit retains the pin markers it has. Once this has been done, continue to test to see if the unit obeys its order in the usual way, even if they have lost their last pin marker.

### VB Launcher
*Reference: Armies of France (v3) page 1*

The VB launcher attachment for the rifle converts the rifle into a short-range light mortar (Range 6–18", Shots 1, PEN HE, Indirect Fire, HE 1"). The infantryman equipped with the VB launcher can either shoot as a rifleman or as a light mortar.

## 2. Platoon Selectors

| Platoon | Code | Mandatory Slots | Optional Support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rifle Platoon** | `infantry` | 1 Platoon Commander, 2 Infantry Sections | Up to 2 Infantry, Medic, Chaplain, Observers, Snipers, Mortars, Transports |
| **Engineer Platoon** | `engineer` | 1 Platoon Commander, 2 Engineer Sections | Up to 2 Engineers, 3 Flamethrowers, Transports |
| **Recce Platoon** | `recce` | 1 Platoon Commander, 1 Infantry, Recce Rule | Up to 3 Infantry, Observers, Anti-tank, Transports |
| **Heavy Weapons Platoon** | `heavy_weapon` | 1 Platoon Commander, 2 Heavy Weapons | Up to 3 Mortars, 3 MMGs, 3 Anti-tank guns, Transports |
| **Artillery Platoon** | `artillery` | 1 Platoon Commander, 1 Artillery piece | Up to 3 Artillery pieces, Tows, Transports |
| **Armoured Platoon** | `armoured` | 1 Command Vehicle, Cmd Vehicle Rule, 1 Tank | Up to 3 additional Tanks / Armoured Cars |
