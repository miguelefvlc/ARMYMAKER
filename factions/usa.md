## 1. National Army Special Rules

### 🎖️ Fire and Manoeuvre

For every three men who fire a rifle/carbine when a unit shoots, you may add one additional dice to the total of shots fired (these shots are assumed to come from any of the men armed as such). For example four men would add one additional shot, and twelve men would add four shots. (10-2025 errata)

### 🎖️ Air Superiority

When rolling to see if an Airstrike arrives, a US Forward Air Observer may roll two dice and pick the highest result. If a 1 is rolled on either die, the player can choose to discard that die if the other die is successful.

### 🎖️ Modern Communications

When rolling on the Outflanking/Reserve table, US players ignore the -1 modifier to Order tests for units in Reserve/Outflanking.

### 🎖️ Fire and Maneuver

US infantry units ignore the -1 to hit penalty for moving and shooting with M1 Garand rifles and carbines, and BARs (Browning Automatic Rifles).

### 🎖️ Gyro-stabilisers

US tanks equipped with gyro-stabilisers ignore the -1 penalty to hit when advancing and firing their main gun if the crew is Veteran, and suffer only a -1 penalty instead of inability to fire or heavier penalty if Regular.
