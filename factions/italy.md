## 1. National Army Special Rules

### 🎖️ Ferrea Mole, Ferreo Cuore (Iron Hulls, Iron Hearts)

Any Italian vehicle with this special rule in its entry may re-roll failed Advance and Run order tests. This rule does not apply when coming onto the table from reserve or outflanking, and it also does not apply for assaults.

### 🎖️ Alzo Zero! (Open Sights!)

All Italian artillery units may reroll any failed morale checks.

### 🎖️ Mixed Quality

All Inexperienced Italian infantry units have the Green special rule.

### 🎖️ Defensive Strategy

When defending in scenarios with an attacker and defender, the Italian player may place D3 dug-in positions or minefields.

### 🎖️ Avanti Savoia!

Italian units check morale with a modified Morale value depending on the state of the army.
