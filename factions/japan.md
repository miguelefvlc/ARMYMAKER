## 1. National Army Special Rules

### 🎖️ Death Before Dishonour

All Imperial Japanese infantry and artillery units have the Fanatics special rule. In addition, they automatically pass morale checks for being assaulted by enemy tanks (note that artillery pieces are still destroyed as normal).

### 🎖️ Banzai!

A Japanese Officer may be given a special Banzai! order. This counts as a "You Men Snap to Action" with the following exceptions: If the order is given successfully, assign the officer a Run order die. Then ALL infantry units within 6" that haven’t already received an order that turn are issued a Run order. Note that this includes infantry units which cannot normally assault enemies, such as those with the Fixed special rule, or the Self-defence special rule. All infantry units can and will assault into close quarters if given the opportunity. Note that, other than for the initiating officer, no order test is required for any units affected by a Banzai! order. This means that affected units execute their Run orders automatically, without any dice rolled, and as such retain any pin markers they have.

### 🎖️ Lack of Radios

When you give the Command Vehicle special rule to a vehicle that does not have it in its entry for the purposes of choosing a command vehicle for an Armoured Platoon, you can give it the rule for free instead of paying the normal +10pts. If you do so, the vehicle reduces the radius of its Command Vehicle rule from 12" to 6". This represents communication by means of flags or hand signals.
