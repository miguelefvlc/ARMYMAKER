## 1. National Army Special Rules

### 🎖️ Fix Bayonets!

For every three models (irrespective of what armament they carry) in your unit involved in close quarters you may add an additional die when rolling to damage. These attacks are assumed to come from any of the models in the unit. For example, four models would add one additional die.

### 🎖️ Artillery Support

Each British Rifle or Heavy Weapons Platoon may include one Forward Observer (Artillery) for free.

### 🎖️ Come on, Lads!

British Platoon and Company Commanders morale bonus is increased by 1, but any cover save they and the men in their unit receive is reduced by 1. (October 2025 errata)

### 🎖️ Bombardment

When rolling to determine the effects of a Preparatory Bombardment, roll two dice per unit and choose the highest result.
